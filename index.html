Our Mission - From Local Produce to Lasting Impact

UTTAM is an agro-processing enterprise that sources produce directly from local farmers, processes it in its own rural units, and sells it through the 'One Village–One Saleswoman' model. By empowering Uttam Didis to lead local sales, UTTAM uplifts rural women in Jharkhand with sustainable, dignified livelihoods and additional income opportunities—building a self-reliant rural economy, one step at a time.

Procurement from Local Farmers

We source high-quality produce directly from rural farmers, ensuring they receive fair prices and a steady, dignified market for their harvests.

Refining and Processing the Grains

Our Uttam Didis clean, sort, and refine produce in locally-run units, using hygienic, small-scale machinery—ensuring both quality and care in every step

Packaging and Branding

Products are sealed in sanitized, tamper-proof packaging to ensure safety and freshness, and clearly labelled with brand details, nutritional info, and expiry dates for consumer trust

Sale

Our dedicated sales team of Uttam didi’s ensures doorstep delivery to both homes and retail outlets, maintaining personalized service and timely distribution to meet customer needs efficiently
